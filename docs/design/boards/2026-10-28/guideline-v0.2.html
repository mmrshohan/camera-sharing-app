PRODUCT DESIGN GUIDELINE · v0.2 · iOS AND ANDROID · LIVING DOCUMENT

Very minimal. One signature: the ring.

This page is the source of truth for design. Engineers build from it; we refine it here first. iOS leads with native features, Android gets the same brand with its own native conventions.

One job per screenOne obvious action. Everything else is one tap away or gone.
The photo is the colorNeutral surfaces. Blue only where you can tap. Soft tints only for status.
Honest by defaultEvery state shows an icon and words. Nothing implies a photo is safe before it is.

01

The ring

The camera shutter, turned into the product’s signature control. Rings are for the camera moment: borrowing, lending, capturing, and photos on their way. Decisions inside panels use plain capsule buttons.
