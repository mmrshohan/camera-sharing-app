## Recommendation

Keep the native mobile apps, one-recipient model, encrypted temporary relay and modular backend. Authorize no production build yet. The concept is plausible, but four production-blocking design gaps need resolution: durable cloud acknowledgment, expiry/late-upload cleanup, lender account isolation during handover, and authenticated device-key continuity. None demonstrates an implemented vulnerability: there is no app yet.

A separate ten-business-day M0 can answer camera and platform feasibility questions. It would not approve the full architecture, pass release gates or authorize subsequent development. Claude's independent review has not run successfully; complete it before treating this memo as the requested final cross-tool review. See [review evidence](review-evidence.md), [proposed document changes](planning-amendments.md) and [M0 scope](m0-feasibility-plan.md).

## Feasibility questions, simplifications and missing requirements

**Camera:** excellent output is possible in principle, not established. Disabling host PhotoKit deferred finalization is consistent with privacy, but quality-prioritized capture can block the next shot. Correct the promise that only network/queue/storage constrains capture: add camera readiness, bounded in-flight work and counts for attempted taps, accepted exposures, returned images and durable Prepared records. Measure tap-to-exposure and shot cadence, not just ≤100 ms haptics. [Apple high-resolution guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/304/).

Start M0 with main/front, processed SDR JPEG and explicit 4:3 framing/selfie mirroring rules. “Always sRGB” and “never re-encode” cannot both be assumed for every source. Define color/orientation behavior and compare pre/post-sanitization pixels, profiles and all metadata containers; a hash computed only after sanitation cannot prove the sanitizer preserved quality. Keep HEIF/HDR, auxiliary lenses, OEM extensions and Camera2 optional until evidence justifies them. Android's current Extensions compatibility notice reinforces the need to recheck the pinned stable library. [CameraX Extensions](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/extensions-api).

**Encryption:** retain standard E2EE, fresh photo keys and sender authentication. Simplify **host key storage**: after atomic Prepared persists immutable ciphertext, recipient envelope, signed context and journal, upload/retry should need no host-decryptable photo-key wrapper. Validate this before adopting it. Local last-shot review can remain memory-only; ending/backgrounding clears it. Do not replace E2EE with server-side encryption or a shared long-lived session key the lender retains: those change the privacy boundary.

**Lifecycle/gallery:** keep Received separate from Saved and preserve the explicit uncertain-export state. Decide whether two persistent recipient copies are an intentional product cost. Recommend keeping the encrypted app gallery for MVP, showing used space and providing explicit deletion; never silently evict app-only photos. Add safe gallery-full behavior and account-switch/logout key handling. Two clocks—host capture retention and server reservation retention—are explicit policies, not inherently a contradiction; agree their user-facing explanation rather than silently rewriting them. Correct the offline status “Needs a new approved session”: the actual proposal requires both devices to approve recovery against the original session. State that the server remains trusted for availability/cleanup and static recipient keys do not provide forward secrecy after later private-key compromise. [HPKE security considerations](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc9180/).

**UX:** three destinations do not prove a simple journey. Measure first use including installation/login/username/permissions separately from repeat pairing. Keep QR prominent; username remains a required release path unless the owner explicitly changes scope. Combine role confirmation and verification into one clear consent flow while preserving both parties' approval. Keep recovery detail out of ordinary capture; do not hide risk or force another modal per photograph.

**Economics:** the initial workload implies 4.5 TB/month delivery before retries, about 4.5 GB per daily-active user/month. At the existing illustrative $0.09/GB, that is $0.405/DAU-month for egress alone; at 100,000 DAU, $40,500/month, before other costs. These are linear scenario calculations, not quotes or forecasts. Keep one region, direct ciphertext transfer and a modular monolith; compare storage providers against measured checksum/deletion contracts and regional pricing. No billion-user claim, Kubernetes or multi-region state is justified. [AWS pricing dimensions](https://aws.amazon.com/s3/pricing/).

**Roadmap:** AI can shorten drafting, bounded implementation, test generation and trace analysis. It cannot replace physical exposures, independent review, a real 72-hour soak, participant recruitment or beta samples. Run two native feasibility tracks concurrently; decouple synthetic transfer experiments from full production auth. Compress M0 from three weeks to ten business days only with ready hardware and approximately 37 specialist person-days. Keep 24–32 weeks as the unvalidated release planning range; re-estimate after M0 and the integrated vertical slice instead of claiming an arbitrary AI speedup.
